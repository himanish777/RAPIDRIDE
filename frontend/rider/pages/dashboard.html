Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request a ride?

Enter your pickup and drop locations, select ride type, and click "Request Ride".

How do I cancel a ride?

Click the "Cancel Ride" button on your current ride card. Note: Cancellation charges may apply.

What ride types are available?

We offer Economy, Comfort, Premium, and Shared ride options with different pricing.

How do payments work?

Fares are calculated based on distance and ride type. Payment is processed at the end of the trip.

What should I do in an emergency?

Click the red SOS button in the bottom right corner to alert emergency contacts and authorities.

How do I contact my driver?

Once a driver is assigned, their contact information will appear in the current ride card.

Can I schedule rides in advance?

Yes! Use the "Schedule Ride" button to book rides for later dates and times.

How do I earn reward points?

You earn points with each completed ride. Points can be redeemed for discounts on future rides.
